About your local dog walker

Based in Great Gransden, I offer dedicated dog‑walking for both Great and Little Gransden. I know the local routes, the quiet cut‑throughs, and the best spots for safe off‑lead time (where appropriate).

I walk:

  • Puppies needing short, positive outings
  • Adult dogs who love regular exercise
  • Senior dogs who prefer a slower pace
  • Nervous or shy dogs who need calm handling

My focus is on gentle, consistent routines so your dog feels relaxed and happy every time they go out.

What you can expect:

  • Meet‑and‑greet before the first walk
  • Walks tailored to your dog’s needs and energy
  • Fresh water and towel‑dry if needed after wet walks
  • Updates after each walk (optional)

If you’re at work, away for the day, or just need an extra hand, I’ll make sure your dog gets the exercise and attention they deserve.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you walk dogs? Mostly around Great & Little Gransden—using local footpaths, fields and quiet lanes that are familiar and safe.
Do you walk reactive or nervous dogs? Yes. Reactive or nervous dogs are walked solo, with calm routes and plenty of space from other dogs and people.
Can I meet you before booking? Absolutely. I offer a free meet‑and‑greet in your village so you and your dog can get to know me first.
Do you walk in bad weather? Light rain is fine, but in extreme weather (heatwaves, storms, ice) we’ll discuss shorter walks or rearranging for safety.
How do I pay? Bank transfer or cash, usually weekly or monthly depending on how often your dog is walked.
