Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you walk dogs? Around Great & Little Gransden using safe, familiar routes.
Do you walk reactive dogs? Yes — solo walks only, with calm routes.
Can we meet first? Yes, I offer a free meet‑and‑greet in your village.
Do you walk in bad weather? Light rain is fine; extreme weather may require rescheduling.
How do I pay? Bank transfer or cash, weekly or monthly.
